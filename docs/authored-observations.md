# Authored cultural observations

Qualitative literary, screen and graphic-novel evidence uses the existing actor/profile-owned AuthoredDiscoveryState JSON, independently of its 30 discovery-topic seed slots. The supported intake is an authenticated review followed by an explicit confirmed import; signing in to the existing Cabinet account is required. A staged packet is not persisted preference evidence. No fixed operator, raw SQL or synthetic session substitutes for that boundary.

Each observation keeps its raw title, medium, qualitative reaction, consumption state, identity confidence, reasons, exact source text and message provenance. A provisional title is not a canonical catalogue match. Books, adaptations, graphic novels, animation and games do not share consumption records. Selected next, planned, interested and removed-from-plan do not mean experienced enjoyment. No qualitative reaction, comparison or ordinal ranking becomes a numeric rating. Assistant recommendations remain separately visible and excluded from preference reinforcement.

The existing synthesis snapshot includes eligible owner observations through a small adapter. Complete provenance participates in the authored-state fingerprint, so edits invalidate old synthesis results even when visible wording is unchanged. Existing topic seeds and shortlists remain intact. The supervised Astra prompt preserves medium-specific qualifications, uncertainty and distinct clusters; links remain inferred taste hypotheses, not factual relationships.
