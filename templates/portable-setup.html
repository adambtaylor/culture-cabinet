Before sharing a request

This build uses OpenAI Responses with gpt-6-astra and high reasoning, with a maximum of 2048 output tokens. Response storage is disabled (store=false); tools, background processing and streaming are not requested.

Disabled response storage does not mean zero retention. API data is not used for training by default unless the account opts in. Abuse-monitoring logs may retain request and response content for up to 30 days, with legal and safety exceptions. Prompt caching may retain encrypted representations for up to 24 hours.

This installation’s account opt-in, Zero Data Retention approval and processing region have not been verified. Review the processor’s data controls and the exact request before confirming.

The connection test uses neutral input. Personal Enhance requests need their own source permissions, data-sharing choices and exact request confirmation. Opening this page or saving a key sends neither request.

About and credits

Recording identities from MusicBrainz

The MusicBrainz source uses MusicBrainz recording titles, artist credits and recording identifiers from its CC0 core data. These facts are separate from model-generated recommendations; no audio rights are implied.

An artist-name search matches names, not a verified artist identity. This source supports recordings only. Other categories remain unavailable unless their own permitted source is configured; music results do not establish film or book coverage.

Other source credits

TMDB and Hardcover are unavailable for this demonstration. Their data is not substituted for MusicBrainz results.

Culture Cabinet identifies the source of movie and TV facts separately from any model-generated recommendation.
